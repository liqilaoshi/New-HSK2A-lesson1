生词 New Words

  • ⑤ 次 cì m.
    (indicating how many times or which time) time; occurrence
  • ⑥ 旅游 lǚyóu v.
    travel; make a tour
  • • 第一次 dì-yī cì num.-m.
    first time
  • • 中文 Zhōngwén n.
    Chinese language
  • • 忙 máng adj.
    busy
  • • 找 zhǎo v.
    look for; turn to
